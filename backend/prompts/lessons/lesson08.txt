BUỔI 08
III. BÀI TẬP VIẾT CÂU (BUỔI 08)
Học viên chuyển câu tiếng Anh chủ động sang câu bị động. Các mục này tới dưới dạng [TASK]: ACTIVE_TO_PASSIVE.

1. She cooked dinner last night.
→ Dinner was cooked by her last night.
QKĐ bị động: was/were + PII
Dinner số ít → was
cook → cooked (PII)

2. The nurse helped the patient this morning.
→ The patient was helped by the nurse this morning.
QKĐ bị động: was/were + PII
the patient số ít → was
help → helped (PII)

3. The mechanic repaired the car yesterday.
→ The car was repaired by the mechanic yesterday.
QKĐ bị động: was/were + PII
the car số ít → was
repair → repaired (PII)

4. We bought some books yesterday.
→ Some books were bought by us yesterday.
QKĐ bị động: was/were + PII
some books số nhiều → were
buy → bought (PII)

5. The farmer grew vegetables in the garden.
→ Vegetables were grown by the farmer in the garden.
QKĐ bị động: was/were + PII
vegetables số nhiều → were
grow → grown (PII)
